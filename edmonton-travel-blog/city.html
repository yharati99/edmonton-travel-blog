Edmonton at a Glance

Edmonton is a vibrant city that combines natural scenery with urban attractions, making it a great destination for short trips or weekend travel. The river valley offers extensive trails and viewpoints that feel surprisingly close to downtown. The city also has lively neighbourhoods with local restaurants, cafés, and shops that are easy to explore on foot.

What Visitors Enjoy

  • The River Valley
  • West Edmonton Mall
  • The Mutart Conservatory
  • Fort Edmonton Park

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit Edmonton is during the summer months, from June through to August, when the weather is warm and the city comes alive with many festivals. This season is perfect for exploring the River Valley trails and enjoying the long daylight hours. If you prefer winter activities, visiting between December and February offers great opportunities for skiing, skating, and viewing Northern Lights.

One-Day Itinerary

  1. Morning: Start your day with a walk or bike ride through the River Valley trails.
  2. Mid-Day: Visit the Muttart Conservatory to explore the unique glass pyramids and diverse indoor plants.
  3. Afternoon: Head to Old Strathcona and Whyte Avenue (82 Ave) for local shopping, old architecture, and a late lunch.
  4. Evening: Catch a sunset from the High Level Bridge Streetcar or enjoy dinner at one of downtowns highly-rated local restaurants.
